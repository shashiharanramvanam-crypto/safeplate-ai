🛡️ Why Open Innovation Matters for Food Allergies

1. Health Privacy & Non-Negotiable Medical Sovereignty:
Anaphylaxis, severe Celiac auto-immune reactions, and medical allergy records are among the most sensitive personal data a human can have. Pushing your roommate's medical diagnoses and daily dietary consumption into closed commercial cloud APIs allows private health profiles to be ingested, mined, and shared across ad networks. With local Google Gemma 2, all inference stays entirely on your own device's RAM.


2. Deterministic Safety Guards over Cloud Hallucination:
General-purpose cloud LLMs frequently "hallucinate" food safety. They will casually suggest Worcester sauce for a gluten-free meal or pesto for a nut-free meal, with devastating health consequences. SafePlate AI pairs local Gemma 2 with an air-gapped deterministic allergen rulebook that screens and verifies every single ingredient before recommendation.


3. Works in Air-Gapped Kitchens & Rural Cabins:
When cooking at an off-grid vacation rental, camping, or checking ingredients in a subterranean grocery aisle with zero cell reception, cloud APIs fail. SafePlate AI runs 100% locally with zero internet dependency.
